Herb or Spice

Boldo

About This Entry

Boldo is a Chilean leaf herb with camphor-eucalyptus aroma used in empanadas and digestifs — its bold herbal note pairs with Carmenère, rustic reds, and mineral whites.

Wine Pairing Explanation

Primary wine styles

Why These Wines Work

Sauvignon Blanc — Boldo bitter camphor leaf suits high-acid Sauvignon Blanc in Chilean herbal infusion contexts.
