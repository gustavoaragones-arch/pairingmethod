Vegetable

Broccoli

About This Entry

Broccoli is a versatile brassica floret with green sulfur character — steamed, roasted, or with cheese, it pairs with Sauvignon Blanc and unoaked Chardonnay.

Wine Pairing Explanation

Primary wine styles

  • sauvignon blanc

Why These Wines Work

Sauvignon Blanc — Broccoli brassica character pairs with high-acid Sauvignon Blanc without oak masking.

Grassy — Broccoli sulfur aligns with grassy Sauvignon Blanc when brassica intensity is managed by acid.
