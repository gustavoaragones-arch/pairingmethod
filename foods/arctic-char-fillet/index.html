Arctic Char Fillet

About This Entry

Arctic char is salmonid with salmon-like fat and trout-like delicacy. Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and dry Riesling suit herb and butter preparations.

Beginner Guide

Farm-raised char is widely available; pink flesh cooks like salmon.

FAQ

What is Arctic Char Fillet?
Farm-raised char is widely available; pink flesh cooks like salmon.
How should Arctic Char Fillet be cooked for best results?
Do not overcook lean fillets; shellfish should be opaque and firm. Rest fish briefly before serving.
