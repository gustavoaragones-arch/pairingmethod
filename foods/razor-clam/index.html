Razor Clam

About This Entry

Razor clam is elongated Pacific and Atlantic bivalve — quick grill or ceviche. High-acid whites, Txakoli, and citrus-driven pairings suit delicate meat.

Beginner Guide

Sand must be purged before cooking; brief heat only.

FAQ

What is Razor Clam?
Sand must be purged before cooking; brief heat only.
How should Razor Clam be cooked for best results?
Do not overcook lean fillets; shellfish should be opaque and firm. Rest fish briefly before serving.
