Protein Food

Baby Back Ribs

About This Entry

Baby back ribs curve from the spine with leaner meat and shorter bones than spare ribs. Popular in American BBQ — pair with the same wine logic as spare ribs: fruit-forward reds and sparkling.

Beginner Guide

Baby backs cook faster than spare ribs because they are smaller and leaner. Do not confuse with beef short ribs.

FAQ

What is Baby Back Ribs?
Baby backs cook faster than spare ribs because they are smaller and leaner.
How should Baby Back Ribs be cooked?
Cook to safe internal temperature; richer cuts tolerate slow cooking, smoke, and braising that build pairing-friendly flavor.
