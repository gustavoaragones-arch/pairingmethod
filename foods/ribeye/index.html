Protein Food

Ribeye Steak

About This Entry

Ribeye is a premium beef cut from the rib section with abundant marbling and rich flavor. High fat and protein make it a classic match for full-bodied red wines with firm tannin, which bind with lipids and soften the wine's structure.

Beginner Guide

Ribeye comes from the upper rib — look for even white marbling (fat streaks) through red muscle. It is one of the richest, most forgiving steak cuts for bold red wine pairings.

FAQ

What is Ribeye Steak?
Ribeye comes from the upper rib — look for even white marbling (fat streaks) through red muscle.
How should Ribeye Steak be cooked for best results?
Rich cuts tolerate medium to medium-rare; render fat slowly on roasts and braises. Rest before serving so juices redistribute.
