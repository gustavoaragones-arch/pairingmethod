Ground Pork

About This Entry

Ground pork forms sausages, dumplings, meatballs, and stir-fries across global cuisines. Fat content drives pairing — richer preparations want acidity; spiced dishes suit off-dry and aromatic whites.

Beginner Guide

Ground pork must be cooked to safe internal temperature. Often blended with other meats or seasoned heavily in recipes.
