Protein Food

Bacon

About This Entry

Bacon is cured and smoked pork belly, ubiquitous as a flavoring and breakfast protein. Salt, smoke, and fat pair with sparkling wine, off-dry whites, and avoid high-tannin reds unless balanced in a dish.

Beginner Guide

American bacon is streaky belly; British back bacon is a different cut. Render slowly for even crispness.

FAQ

What is Bacon?
American bacon is streaky belly; British back bacon is a different cut.
How should Bacon be cooked?
Cook to safe internal temperature; richer cuts tolerate slow cooking, smoke, and braising that build pairing-friendly flavor.
