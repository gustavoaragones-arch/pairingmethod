Protein Food

Wild Boar Shoulder

About This Entry

Wild boar shoulder is collagen-rich — ideal for ragù, stews, and pulled game preparations. Long cooking and fat render pair with Barolo, Aglianico, and hearty southern Italian reds.

Beginner Guide

Shoulder rewards patience. Low oven or Dutch oven braise until shreddable.

FAQ

What is Wild Boar Shoulder?
Shoulder rewards patience.
How should Wild Boar Shoulder be cooked for best results?
Lean game benefits from careful doneness — avoid overcooking loins; braise tougher cuts. Rest before slicing.
