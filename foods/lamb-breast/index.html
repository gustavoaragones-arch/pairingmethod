Protein Food

Lamb Breast

About This Entry

Lamb breast is a thin, fatty cut from the underside — rolled, stuffed, and slow-cooked in British and Mediterranean cooking. Rich fat pairs with acidic whites, off-dry Riesling, and soft tannin reds.

Beginner Guide

Breast has layers of meat and fat. Slow roast or braise to render fat; crisp skin if oven-finished.

FAQ

What is Lamb Breast?
Breast has layers of meat and fat.
How should Lamb Breast be cooked for best results?
Match cooking method to cut — quick heat for tender muscles, slow moist heat for collagen-rich cuts. Rest roasts before slicing.
