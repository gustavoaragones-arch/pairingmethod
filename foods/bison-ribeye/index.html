Protein Food

Bison Ribeye

About This Entry

Bison ribeye is a lean rib-section steak — beef-like but sweeter and lower in fat. Tannin-friendly protein still pairs with Cabernet and Syrah when cooked medium-rare with rested juices.

Beginner Guide

Cooks faster than beef ribeye due to low fat. Pull earlier than beef equivalents.

FAQ

What is Bison Ribeye?
Cooks faster than beef ribeye due to low fat.
How should Bison Ribeye be cooked for best results?
Lean game benefits from careful doneness — avoid overcooking loins; braise tougher cuts. Rest before slicing.
