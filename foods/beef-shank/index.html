Protein Food

Beef Shank

About This Entry

Beef shank is a lean, sinewy leg cut built for braising — osso buco and soup bones share this muscle. Collagen melts into silky broth; pair with medium reds or robust whites when the dish includes tomato, herbs, or reduced wine sauces.

Beginner Guide

Shank is cross-cut with a marrow bone in the center. It is never a quick-cook steak — think stews, braises, and bone marrow richness.

FAQ

What is Beef Shank?
Shank is cross-cut with a marrow bone in the center.
How should Beef Shank be cooked for best results?
Lean cuts benefit from medium-rare to medium doneness, resting before slice, and slicing against the grain — avoid overcooking which dries the meat and makes tannic wines taste harsh.
