Protein Food

Skirt Steak

About This Entry

Skirt steak is a thin, flavorful diaphragm cut prized for fajitas and quick high-heat searing. Moderate fat and strong beef character suit Malbec, Tempranillo, and zesty reds that complement spice and lime.

Beginner Guide

Inside skirt and outside skirt exist — outside is more tender and prized. Marinate briefly, cook hot and fast, rest, then slice against the grain.

FAQ

What is Skirt Steak?
Inside skirt and outside skirt exist — outside is more tender and prized.
How should Skirt Steak be cooked for best results?
Cook to medium-rare or medium depending on thickness; rest 5–10 minutes and slice against the grain when the muscle grain is visible.
