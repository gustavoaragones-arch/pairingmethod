Protein Food

Turkey Thigh

About This Entry

Turkey thigh is darker, juicier meat than breast — excellent braised or roasted. Higher fat suits medium reds, earthy whites, and wines with savory depth.

Beginner Guide

Thighs are forgiving for holiday cooks — harder to overcook than breast. Braise in wine for built-in pairing synergy.

How should Turkey Thigh be cooked for best results?
