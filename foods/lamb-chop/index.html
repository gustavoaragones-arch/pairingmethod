Protein Food

Lamb Chop

About This Entry

Lamb chops are individual portions cut from the loin or rib — fast-cooking and grill-friendly. Classic match for Cabernet Franc, Merlot, and Mediterranean reds with herbal notes.

Beginner Guide

Rib chops come from the rack; loin chops from the loin. Both cook quickly — aim for pink center unless recipe calls for well-done.

FAQ

What is Lamb Chop?
Rib chops come from the rack; loin chops from the loin.
How should Lamb Chop be cooked for best results?
Match cooking method to cut — quick heat for tender muscles, slow moist heat for collagen-rich cuts. Rest roasts before slicing.
