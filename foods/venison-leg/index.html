Protein Food

Venison Leg

About This Entry

Venison leg is the haunch roasted whole or boneless — holiday game centerpiece. Herb crusts and juniper sauces match Bordeaux, Rioja, and medium tannin reds.

Beginner Guide

Bone-in leg has more flavor; boneless is easier to carve. Lard or barding helps lean haunch roasts.

FAQ

What is Venison Leg?
Bone-in leg has more flavor; boneless is easier to carve.
How should Venison Leg be cooked for best results?
Lean game benefits from careful doneness — avoid overcooking loins; braise tougher cuts. Rest before slicing.
