Protein Food

Coppa

About This Entry

Coppa is dry-cured muscle from the neck/shoulder, sliced thin on charcuterie boards. Spiced, marbled, and savory — pairs with Lambrusco, dry rosé, and light rustic reds.

Beginner Guide

Capicola in US delis is often coppa. Served thinly sliced, not cooked — pairing refers to charcuterie context.

FAQ

What is Coppa?
Capicola in US delis is often coppa.
How should Coppa be cooked?
Cook to safe internal temperature; richer cuts tolerate slow cooking, smoke, and braising that build pairing-friendly flavor.
