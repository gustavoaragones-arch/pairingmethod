Baby Octopus

About This Entry

Baby octopus is bite-sized cephalopod — marinated and grilled. Greek and Japanese izakaya styles pair with Assyrtiko, sake, and light reds.

Beginner Guide

Tenderizes faster than large octopus; high heat sear after brief cook.
