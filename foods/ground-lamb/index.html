Protein Food

Ground Lamb

About This Entry

Ground lamb is trim and muscle minced for burgers, kofta, moussaka, and shepherd's pie. Fat ratio and spice drive pairing — mint and cumin preparations suit aromatic whites and medium reds.

Beginner Guide

Fresh ground lamb can be lean; some blends add fat for juiciness. Cook to safe temperature; burgers can stay pink in the center if sourced and handled safely.

FAQ

What is Ground Lamb?
Fresh ground lamb can be lean; some blends add fat for juiciness.
How should Ground Lamb be cooked for best results?
Match cooking method to cut — quick heat for tender muscles, slow moist heat for collagen-rich cuts. Rest roasts before slicing.
