Protein Food

Turkey Breast

About This Entry

Turkey breast is lean white meat — roasted whole or sliced for sandwiches. Mild flavor suits crisp whites, dry rosé, and light Pinot Noir; gravy and stuffing add pairing richness.

Beginner Guide

Bone-in breast roasts more evenly; boneless cooks faster but dries easily. Rest and slice against the grain.

FAQ

What is Turkey Breast?
Bone-in breast roasts more evenly; boneless cooks faster but dries easily.
How should Turkey Breast be cooked for best results?
Cook to safe internal temperature for poultry; render duck and goose fat slowly. Rest before carving or serving.
