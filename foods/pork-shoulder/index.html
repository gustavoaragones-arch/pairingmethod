Pork Shoulder

About This Entry

Pork shoulder is a collagen-rich cut ideal for pulled pork, carnitas, and long smoking. Fat and smoke marry with Zinfandel, Grenache, and rustic medium reds with spice.

Beginner Guide

Boston butt is actually the upper shoulder — not the rear of the hog. Low and slow cooking breaks down connective tissue into tender pulled meat.

FAQ

What is Pork Shoulder?

How should Pork Shoulder be cooked?
Cook to safe internal temperature; richer cuts tolerate slow cooking, smoke, and braising that build pairing-friendly flavor.
