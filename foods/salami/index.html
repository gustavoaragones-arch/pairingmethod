Salami

About This Entry

Salami is dry-cured fermented sausage — savory, garlicky, and fat-rich. Structured reds, Lambrusco, and off-dry whites suit charcuterie boards.

Beginner Guide

Slice thin for boards; cooking optional in pasta and pizza.
