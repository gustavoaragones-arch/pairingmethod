Beans

About This Group

Beans are the largest and most versatile legume group, from creamy cannellini to hearty kidney and pinto beans, forming the backbone of stews, chilis, and slow-simmered dishes worldwide.

Dried and fresh culinary beans used in stews, soups, and pulse cookery around the world, including kidney bean, black bean, pinto bean, navy bean, and cannellini bean.
