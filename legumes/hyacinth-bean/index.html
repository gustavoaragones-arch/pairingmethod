Hyacinth Bean

About This Entry

Hyacinth beans, or lablab, are a purple-podded legume common across South and Southeast Asian and East African cooking, with young pods offering a mild, slightly sweet flavor — that gentle sweetness pairs with the stone-fruit character of Viognier.
