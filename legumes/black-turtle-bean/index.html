Legume

Black Turtle Bean

About This Entry

Black turtle beans are the small, glossy black bean common in Cuban and Brazilian cooking, valued for a dense, creamy texture in feijoada and rice-and-beans dishes — their richness matches a dark-fruited Malbec.
