About This Entry

Chimichurri is a canonical Argentine herb-oil sauce for grilled beef and chorizo ingredient in global cookery — its acidity, richness, and pairing behavior pair with structured whites, aromatic rosés, and spice-friendly reds when used as a finishing or table sauce.
