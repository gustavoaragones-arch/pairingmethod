Poultry

About This Group

Chicken, turkey, duck, goose, and quail — species-tagged cuts for filtering, pairing, and future dish connectivity.

Poultry spans chicken, turkey, duck, goose, and quail — everyday to celebratory proteins with wide pairing range. Species and cut (white vs dark meat, fat content) matter more than tannin alone for wine matching.

FAQ

Why does poultry pair with both white and red wine?
Lean breast suits crisp whites and rosé; rich duck, thigh meat, and roast preparations handle light to medium reds.
How does species metadata help?
The species field filters chicken, turkey, duck, goose, and quail without adding hierarchy depth — enabling analytics and search across a flat entity model.
