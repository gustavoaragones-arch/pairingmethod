Protein Group

Veal

About This Group

Veal's delicate, mild flavor across cutlets, chops, loin, and shank makes it a classic match for lighter, more nuanced European wine pairings.

Veal is young cattle (Bos taurus) — pale, delicate, and lean compared to beef. Italian and French traditions center on cutlets, osso buco, and braised breast; mild flavor suits elegant whites and lighter reds.

FAQ

Why does veal pair with white wine more often than beef?
Mild flavor and lower fat suit crisp whites and light reds; heavy tannic reds can overpower delicate veal.
What is osso buco?
Osso buco is braised cross-cut veal shank — marrow-rich, traditionally served with gremolata and Barolo or Barbaresco.
